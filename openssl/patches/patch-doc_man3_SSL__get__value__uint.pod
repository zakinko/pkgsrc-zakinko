+=item B<SSL_VALUE_DTLS_LISTENER_CID_LEN> (DTLS listener object)
+
+Generic value. The length in bytes of the DTLS 1.2 Connection ID (RFC 9146)
+that the listener gives each connection it creates, from 1 to
+B<SSL_DTLS_CID_MAX_LEN>; 0, the default, gives none. A connection whose client
+negotiates the "connection_id" extension then receives records carrying that
+CID, and the listener routes such records by the CID rather than by the
+address they came from, so the connection survives a change of the client's
+address. The CIDs are random. The length cannot be changed while connections
+that have a CID exist. See L<SSL_set1_dtls_cid(3)>.
